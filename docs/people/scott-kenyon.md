## Biography

Scott Kenyon is a Programme Lead within the School of Technology at BPP, specialising in software engineering, software testing, quality engineering and DevOps.

He works closely with employers, learners and industry partners to design and deliver programmes that develop practical workplace skills and support career progression within the technology sector.
