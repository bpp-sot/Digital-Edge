## Biography

Mo Warsame leads the Data and AI Level 3 to Level 6 apprenticeships, creating AI tools that automate content generation and streamline assessment marking. He is Head of AI Learning for Tech Apps & HE, working on tools such as ConCraftr, FeedbackHub, and MarkAssist that put questions of human judgement and ownership at the centre of how AI gets used in education.

A former senior data analyst, Mo's expertise spans advanced analytics, data insight, and application development, with strong Python, R, and SQL skills. He holds an MSc in Applied Econometrics and a first-class BSc in Business Economics, and won Media Week's Award for Best Use of Econometrics for an investment optimisation tool.
