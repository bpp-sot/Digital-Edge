## Biography

Catherine has worked in education and apprenticeships for 12 years, working through community, product, learner experience, and content roles. She has always had a passion for prioritising the learning experience and supporting clients.

Currently, Catherine focuses on supporting clients as they define their learning solutions, understanding how we talk about the impact of learning, and building out BPP's Tech Apps & HE product proposition.

Catherine's interests include learning experience, new and emerging technologies, sustainability, and women in tech.
