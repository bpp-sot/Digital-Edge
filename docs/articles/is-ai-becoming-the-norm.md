# Is AI… Becoming the Norm?

{{ article_hero(page) }}

{{ article_summary(page) }}

{{ article_takeaways(page) }}

Today I found myself chatting to AI on my phone while walking the dog. Didn't think twice about it.

<p class="de-article-interjection">The passers-by did. 😊</p>

But it got me thinking: at what point does new technology stop being new and just become the norm?

There's a fair concern that the more AI does for us, the less we'll think for ourselves. I think the opposite could be true.

<p class="de-article-interjection">The real skill isn't getting the answer anymore. It's knowing whether to trust it.</p>

<figure class="de-trust-check">
  <div class="de-trust-check__answer">

    <p class="de-trust-check__verdict">That doesn't make it right.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="de-trust-check__human">
    <p>We still need the human touch:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Curiosity</li>
      <li>Judgement</li>
      <li>Critical thinking</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="de-trust-check__goal">to check it makes sense.</p>
  </div>
</figure>

I didn't start in tech. I came from a health and fitness background, and moving into technology meant a lot of questions and plenty of mistakes.

I wish I'd had AI back then. Being able to ask the question you'd feel silly asking a colleague, and keep going until it clicks, makes learning far less intimidating.

It's something I talk about with my learners a lot.

<figure class="de-evidence-log">
  <div class="de-evidence-log__not">
    <p class="de-evidence-log__entry"><s>"I used AI."</s></p>
    <p class="de-evidence-log__stamp">Simply using AI isn't the evidence.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="de-evidence-log__is">
    <p class="de-evidence-log__lead">What matters is what you did with it.</p>
    <p class="de-evidence-log__if">If it helped you work through a problem:</p>
    <ol>
      <li>What did you question?</li>
      <li>What did you change?</li>
      <li>What difference did it make in your role?</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <figcaption>That's where the real learning is.</figcaption>
</figure>

Soon, saying "I use AI at work" may be as impressive as saying "I use Excel".

<figure class="de-question-swap">

  <p class="de-question-swap__but">It'll be</p>
  <p class="de-question-swap__new">"What can you do differently now that you have it?"</p>
</figure>
