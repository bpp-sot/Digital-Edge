  - cite: "Luke Parkin"
    role: "Degree apprentice"
    text: |
      The DeepRacer event was both really fun and a great learning experience. Prior to the event, we attended some lectures hosted by AWS members explaining how model training works, how AWS is used to set up these training models, and tips and tricks on how to train our models. We were provided the resources and expertise to then go ahead and start training our models.

      We trained a deep learning model in a virtual simulator to race a car around a track. After hours of training several models, we selected the best of our team and submitted them. On the day, our team met up and we discussed strategy, as we weren't sure how the models would perform in the real world with real camera input, rather than simulated cameras.

      We were one of the first racers to go, and were cautiously optimistic, however that quickly went out the window when our car started throwing itself off the track! Fortunately, though, we had two chances to race. So in between some of the amazingly informative lectures put on by AWS staff at the event, on both technical and project management topics, we looked at our other models, made some fixes and gave it another go a few hours later.

      Our models did much better this time, and we secured a much better spot on the leaderboard. It was a really fun event and I would definitely encourage people to give it a go if you have the opportunity!
