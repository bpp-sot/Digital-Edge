For the second consecutive year, BPP provided a co-host for the event. Sam Fletcher, currently completing his Level 4 Software Developer apprenticeship with Firebrand, joined LJ on stage and brought energy, wit and charm to the evening, keeping the audience entertained and engaged. Well done Sam, and to top it off, he managed to bag an award himself.

We're incredibly proud of every finalist. Reaching this stage is an achievement in itself: it takes a compelling written nomination, reviewed by a BCS panel, followed by an interview.
