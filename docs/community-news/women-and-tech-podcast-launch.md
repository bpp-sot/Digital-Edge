## A Series Built by the Community

This first episode marks the start of a community-driven Women + Tech series where anyone, from first-day learners to seasoned tech pros, can get involved. Whether you want to be a guest, pitch a topic, or even try hosting, this is your space.

We're building a podcast series for everyone who cares about women and tech: a place to share discoveries, ask big questions, and explore what it truly means to find (and use) your voice in tech.
