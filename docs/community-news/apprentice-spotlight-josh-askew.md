interview:
  - question: "Tell us about your background and career journey so far"
    answer: |
      I have always been interested in all areas of IT as a hobby, such as trying out programming, graphic design, building PCs, web development and playing video games. I studied Computer Science at both GCSE and A-level and felt that looking for an apprenticeship would be a good fit for me.

      After finishing Sixth Form, and a few months of searching, I landed my apprenticeship in my hometown with Integral IT, with Firebrand Training. Over the 14 months I have been with the company, the opportunities, freedom and support I have been given have been immense. I started in First Line Support, was promoted to Second Line Support, and now, on completing my apprenticeship, I am the business's Service Desk Lead. In this time, my technical knowledge and skillset have improved at a massive rate. I am now supporting new apprentices who come into the business, managing entire projects independently and meeting all our Service Desk requirements with escalated tickets.

      The additional certifications provided by the Firebrand apprenticeship were an excellent experience. I always looked forward to driving to the training centre on the four occasions I visited. The training was great, and I took each vendor exam at the end of each course and passed every certification.
  - question: "How and why did you choose to study an apprenticeship?"
    answer: |
      I chose to study an apprenticeship as I liked the 'hands-on', real work experience. I feel if I had gone to university (as that was always my alternative), I would still be the same person at the end, stepping into an entry-level IT job with no corporate experience under my belt. The freedom of the apprenticeship is perfect for how I wanted to work.
  - question: "How did completing your apprenticeship benefit you?"
    answer: |
      It gave me the certification for all my hard work and proof of the skills I have gained over the course. The certifications I gained, as well as my fast development, have helped me progress through the business internally. As mentioned, I have also gained a promotion.
  - question: "What was your favourite part of the apprenticeship?"
    answer: |
      The training and instructors were all great, and I passed all of the vendor exams first time. They were a brilliant break from my role to focus on the certifications.

      My learning mentor, <a href="../staff-spotlight-steve-alami/">Steve Alami</a>, was another highlight of my apprenticeship. The check-in meetings, progress reviews and support provided by Steve were perfect.
  - question: "Did any of your work have a positive impact on a client or your local community?"
    answer: |
      The positive impact on the technical side of things has been significant. One of our clients named me in one of their Google reviews.
