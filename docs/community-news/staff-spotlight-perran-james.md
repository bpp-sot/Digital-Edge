interview:
  - question: "Please explain your job role"
    answer: |
      My job role involves working closely with learners to help them achieve their apprenticeship. My responsibilities include keeping them aware of timeline events, checking their welfare, keeping their progress moving in the right direction and helping them when they may be struggling or need help or guidance. I also have regular 1:1s with them and every 8 weeks also with their managers to ensure we share 360-degree feedback in a formal manner. It is also important that all learners' individual needs are accommodated, and this can include adapting to their additional learning needs to ensure any medical conditions are supported. I feel everyone has the right to learn, but we need to ensure despite their individual needs, they have the opportunity to do so and also flourish at the same time. Working to deadlines and compliance frameworks is also an important part of my job.
  - question: "What inspired you to work in education?"
    answer: |
      I have worked in the IT industry for over 36 years now and felt compelled to pass on my own experiences and knowledge onto those who are following a similar career pathway. I get a lot of satisfaction coaching especially younger learners and seeing them achieve not only their apprenticeships but also their aspirations.
  - question: "What's the most memorable moment you've had doing your current role?"
    answer: |
      I was originally brought in to troubleshoot a stakeholder contract that had been adversely impacted by the effects of the COVID-19 lockdowns. Apprentices were past their original end dates, demoralised and disillusioned. I was delighted to have turned this situation around to a positive outcome for those affected, with all of the learners achieving their apprenticeship and in many cases securing distinctions too. It was hard work and there were many sleepless nights worrying about whether I could do it without letting people down, but with hard work, I was very happy with the outcome. Many of those affected learners are still in touch with me to this day and tell me how much the apprenticeship has changed their life for the better.
  - question: "Whilst it might be hard to remember a time when you weren't dedicating your life to the BPP Education Group, please tell us about the most interesting job you have had"
    answer: |
      I was an apprenticeship assessor for IT Technician, customer service and business administration and I worked very closely with the Army and Royal Navy and was often in the tracked vehicles and on board ships doing observations, which was an enjoyable workplace. During my time supporting the Army with their apprenticeship in particular, one of my own apprentices achieved Army Apprentice of the Year 2018.
