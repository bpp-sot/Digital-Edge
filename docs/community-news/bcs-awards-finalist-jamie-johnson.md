    answer: |
      The award ceremony was a brilliant day. There were lots of amazing people in a room, and I got to meet people in person who I previously knew by name.
  - question: "What inspired you to pursue a digital apprenticeship?"
    answer: |
      I pursued a digital apprenticeship in data because I like maths, statistics and computer science.
  - question: "What has been the most rewarding part of your apprenticeship journey so far?"
    answer: |
      Running a community of over 250 people to try and make apprenticeships a better place for future candidates.
  - question: "Can you share a project or achievement you're particularly proud of?"
    answer: |
      When I first joined my company, they had just started a complex model development project. By the time of writing this, the project is nearly complete. I'm proud to see how my development has also related to the development of the project.
  - question: "What skills have you developed that you didn't expect to?"
    answer: |
      Communication. Before I joined an apprenticeship, I was honestly the most introverted person, but now I speak in front of hundreds of people on a regular basis.
  - question: "Who would you like to thank or acknowledge for supporting your journey?"
    answer: |
      The apprenticeship team within the company, who have supported me with my apprenticeship journey and all my ideas. My team also allow me to do all the things I do other than my job.
